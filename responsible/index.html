Play Responsibly

Mega Millions is a pool between friends. The fun is in waiting for the ball to drop — not in how much is at stake.

Agree on the stakes first. Decide what each ticket costs and what each prize tier pays before the first drawing.

Only play with what you can spare. Never wager money you will miss at the end of the month.

A pool is not an investment. The odds of matching five numbers plus the Mega Ball are roughly 1 in 290 million.

Never chase losses. Raising the stakes after a bad drawing is the most common sign that things are getting out of hand.

No players under 18. They can watch the drawing, but they do not join the pool.

If the game has stopped being fun for you or someone close to you, it is worth talking to a professional. In the U.S., the National Problem Gambling Helpline is available at 1-800-GAMBLER.
